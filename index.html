About Me

At my core,
I'm an accountant.

I hold a BBA in Accounting and Finance and began my career as a Gas Volume Accountant, where I developed a strong foundation in the midstream energy industry.

Before that, I worked in a variety of corporate roles while putting myself through college, including administrative, customer service, bookkeeping, and office management positions.
